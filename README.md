# SEASTRONG · Southern Belize site map

Interactive map of candidate mangrove, seagrass and coral restoration sites in southern Belize (Port Honduras, Sapodilla Cayes and the southern lagoon), prepared for the SEASTRONG pre-visit with TIDE and Healthy Reefs, Nov–Dec 2026.

**Open the map:** https://reef-scuba.github.io/seastrong-belize-site-map/

## What's on it

- **Clarity-screened sites R1–R13.** Each site has a mangrove, seagrass and coral point, picked so the water is clear enough for repeated dive work. The Sites tab lets you sort and filter them; the About tab in the map explains the scoring.
- **Earlier scouting layers:** corridors C1–C11, mangrove plot candidates M1–M12, seagrass plot candidates S1–S10, and the original figures.
- **Habitat:** Allen Coral Atlas seagrass and coral/algae; Global Mangrove Watch v3 mangroves.
- **Water clarity:** Sentinel-2 turbidity, 2020–2025, by season, plus rivers and river mouths.
- **Depth:** regional bathymetry and depth contours. These come from a ~460 m grid and don't resolve reef tops.
- **Reef monitoring:** AGRRA benthic and coral health surveys, loaded live from AGRRA's public services.
- **Protected areas:** WDPA boundaries, loaded live from UNEP-WCMC.
- **Daily satellite imagery:** NASA GIBS (MODIS, VIIRS, PACE, Harmonized Landsat–Sentinel).

## Shortlists and notes

Starring sites and writing notes works in the **Shortlist** tab. These are saved **only in your own browser**, on that device. To share a shortlist, use **Export CSV**; its columns match the Site register tab of the SEASTRONG field workbook.

## Status and caveats

This is a preliminary, desk-based site screen. Site locations, scores and clarity estimates need checking in the field with partners before any decision. The About tab in the map lists the method and the main caveats.
