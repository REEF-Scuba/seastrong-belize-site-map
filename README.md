# SEASTRONG · Southern Belize site map

Interactive map of candidate mangrove, seagrass and coral restoration sites in southern Belize (Port Honduras, the Sapodilla Cayes and the lagoon north to Placencia), prepared for the SEASTRONG pre-visit with TIDE and Healthy Reefs for Healthy People, 24 November to 3 December 2026.

**Open the map:** https://reef-scuba.github.io/seastrong-belize-site-map/

## Contents

- **Clarity-screened sites R1 to R13.** Each site has a mangrove, seagrass and coral point, selected for water clear enough for repeated scuba-based work. The Sites tab lets you sort and filter them; the About tab in the map explains the scoring.
- **Earlier scouting layers:** corridors C1 to C11, mangrove plot candidates M1 to M12, seagrass plot candidates S1 to S10, and the first-pass figures.
- **Habitat:** Allen Coral Atlas seagrass and coral/algae; Global Mangrove Watch v3 mangroves.
- **Water clarity:** Sentinel-2 turbidity, 2020 to 2025, by season, plus rivers and river mouths.
- **Depth:** regional bathymetry and depth contours. They come from a grid of about 460 m cells and do not resolve reef tops.
- **Reef monitoring:** AGRRA benthic and coral health surveys, loaded live from AGRRA's public services.
- **Protected areas:** WDPA boundaries, loaded live from UNEP-WCMC.
- **Daily satellite imagery:** NASA GIBS (MODIS, VIIRS, PACE, Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2).

## Shortlists and notes

Sites can be starred and annotated in the **Shortlist** tab. These are saved **only in your own browser**, on that device. To share a shortlist, use **Export CSV**; its columns match the Site register tab of the SEASTRONG field workbook.

## Status and caveats

This is a preliminary, desk-based site screen. Site locations, scores and clarity estimates need checking in the field with partners before any decision. The About tab in the map lists the method and the main caveats.
